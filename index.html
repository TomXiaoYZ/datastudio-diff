See exactly what changed in your Data Studio report.

Snapshot any Data Studio (Looker Studio) report, compare two snapshots, and get a page-by-page, chart-by-chart change list: dimensions, metrics, filters, data sources. Runs in your browser. Your report never leaves your machine.

Why

  • Version history tells you who and when, not what. It doesn't show which chart, field or filter changed, and restoring rolls back the whole report.
  • Fix only what broke. Get a list of the few charts that changed and put them back by hand, without losing everyone else's edits.
  • 100% local. No server account, no uploads. Snapshots are stored in your browser.
